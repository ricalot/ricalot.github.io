The Principal

Richard "Ric" Halse

With a comprehensive career dedicated to the advancement of regional Western Australia, Richard Halse brings extensive executive experience to regional advisory.

As a former Chief Executive Officer for regional Shires, Ric understands the complexities of regional governance and the regulatory frameworks governing local government administrations. He has a proven track record of supporting major funding initiatives, strengthening governance frameworks, and facilitating complex regional projects.

Ric brings firsthand insight into the strategic expectations of regional communities and the vital importance of the relationship between governance boards, elected members, and the executive.

Through Regional Integrated Consultancy, Ric offers high-level strategic and governance advice to Local Governments and Native Title organizations—delivering counsel that is legislatively sound and practically viable.
